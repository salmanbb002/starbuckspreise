### maps_search

### maps_nearby

### maps_routes

[Obtener indicaciones y mostrar rutas en Google Maps](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/144339?hl=es) — accessed 2026-09-13.

### maps_location

[Encontrar tu ubicación y mejorar la precisión en Google Maps](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/2839911?hl=es) — accessed 2026-09-13.
Location permissions and the blue location indicator help check the origin; accuracy can vary.

### maps_offline

[Descargar zonas y navegar sin conexión en Google Maps](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6291838?hl=es) — accessed 2026-09-13.
Downloadable areas; offline restrictions include no walking/cycling/transit directions and no live traffic. Do not promise fresh opening hours offline.

### mop

[FAQ zu Mobile Order & Pay](https://www.starbucks.de/de/faq-mop) — accessed 2026-09-13.
App and Rewards sign-in required; participating stores identified in locator. Prepared order is collected in chosen store. No additional service fee; product availability and preparation vary.

### card

[FAQ zur Starbucks® Card](https://www.starbucks.de/de/faq-starbucks-card) — accessed 2026-09-13.
Card acceptance limited to participating German stores; licensed exceptions. Does not establish universal cross-border account/card interoperability.

### food

[FAQ zu Getränken und Speisen](https://www.starbucks.de/de/faq-food-drink) — accessed 2026-09-13.
General menu can have short-term stock gaps. Vegan food and plant drinks available in range. Links to current nutrition and allergens; no medical/allergen-safety inference.

### menu

[Order - Menu | Starbucks](https://www.starbucks.de/de/menu) — accessed 2026-09-13.
Menu categories: espresso, tea, Frappuccino, chocolate; desserts, sandwiches, snacks. No verified branch-specific price list.

### delivery

[Starbucks Delivers](https://www.starbucks.de/de/delivers) — accessed 2026-09-13.

### delivery_faq

Supplemental delivery FAQ; used only where specific text was inspected. Delivery price/availability checked at ordering stage.

## Editorial limitations

No first-hand café visit, phone verification, wait-time measurement or personal author credentials. Opening hours are published listings retrieved on the research date, not a live open/closed status. Branch prices, stock, sockets, seating, parking fees, drive-through availability and international Rewards compatibility are not asserted without evidence. No messages were sent to stores.
